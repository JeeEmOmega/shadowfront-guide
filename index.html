Win the count

Event rules

Station your Flagship near a Vault and occupation fills for the guild with the most surviving Flagships in the area. A bigger lead over second place fills it faster. Damage doesn't move the count. Removing ships does.

1 · Garrison

Enemy-owned? Defeat its garrison. Near a deadline, time the last hit.

2 · Neutral

The Vault resets to neutral. Nobody owns it yet.

3 · Occupy

Hold the count lead until occupation is full.

Seen in battle

Tap a Vault and its bar shows the owner and a percentage, with each guild's fleet count underneath in that guild's colour. On an enemy-owned Vault the percentage is its garrison's health. The count is fleets, not players: each guild counts on its own, and rivals don't add together.

Leave the whale, or turn on it?

Guild tactic

A whale is a top-power player whose fleet hits far harder than most. Weakest-first is a count tactic, not an absolute rule.

Usually: thin the escorts

  • It's tough, but it isn't knocking out our holders.
  • Weak enemy ships in the circle can be finished.

Their count drops. Without its escorts, the whale is easier to deal with later.

Switch: turn on it together

  • It's about to knock out enough of our fleets to flip the count.
  • Or a commander drops the attack signal on it.

Everyone on the call hits it at once. Then back to the count.

Stop hitting it

  • Chasing it pulls our ships out of the circle.
  • We're losing ships faster than it's losing health.
  • It isn't going down, and weak targets are open.

Back inside the rim. Back to the weak ones.

Not a whale? You still count

Guild tactic

Awakening: one segment closer

Event rulesGuild tactic

After 10 minutes, two Lesser Vaults and one Central Vault enter Brief Awakening every 5 minutes, for 4:30. They show the orange badge 02:37 on the map. Whoever owns the Vault when it hits 0:00 gets a large amount of Battle Points.

The minute marks are approximate. The orange timer on your mini-map is what counts.

Time the takedown

Guild tactic

When a rival owns a Vault we want, don't knock out its garrison the moment we can. Knock it out early and they have time to retake it and score before the timer ends. Bring it low, stack our fleets, and finish it late.

Best against a deadline

Use it on Awakening-marked Vaults. On an unmarked Vault they keep scoring every second you wait, so the commander decides.

Watch the third guild

When the garrison falls, whoever has the most fleets in the circle advances. If another guild is bigger there, they take it, not us.

Out of time

Can't finish occupation? Knock it out anyway so it's neutral at 0:00. That denies their bonus unless they retake it first.

Event rules, short

Outer Rim Outpost

You can't be attacked inside its range, and HP restores gradually. Defeated fleets respawn here with a temporary speed boost. Ships are never permanently destroyed.

Repair Modules

Several spawn near a Vault that's about to enter Brief Awakening. Collect them to repair minor damage to your Flagship.

Commander signals

Tap a signal to place it on your selected Flagship, or hold and drag it onto the mini-map. Every ally gets a notification and a map marker.

Unyielding Will

Players who repeatedly fail to defeat enemies get a large boost to their fleet's base attributes. The exact trigger isn't shown.

Squads

Up to 20 members per guild on the battlefield at once. Each squad has up to 20 mains and 10 substitutes.

Fleets per player

3 each, or 4 at Energy Core level 33+. With 20 members on the field that's up to 60 counting fleets per guild.

Guild-reported

Exactly tied fleet count

Reportedly the guild that got there first keeps the edge. Not confirmed yet, and the capture formula isn't known.

Guild-reported

Never exit permanently

A permanent exit frees your slot and you can't re-enter. Leaving temporarily is a separate option.

Guild Battle Points

From Vaults we own, plus Awakening bonuses. They decide the guild ranking. A guild with 0 Battle Points gets no ranking rewards.

Battle Contribution

Personal. Damage converts to Contribution, plus capture and final-hit awards. It doesn't count toward Battle Points or decide who owns a Vault.
