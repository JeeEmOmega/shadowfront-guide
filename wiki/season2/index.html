Hunt points

Every server hunts together toward one goal: Ascendancy Minions and Tribute Vessels.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  • Launch a rally and defeat 1 Deliverer Ark+6,000
  • Defeat 1 Lvl 1–10 Ascendancy Minion+1,000
  • Defeat 1 Lvl 11–20 Ascendancy Minion+1,200
  • Defeat 1 Lvl 21–30 Ascendancy Minion+1,400
  • Defeat 1 Lvl 31–40 Ascendancy Minion+1,600
  • Defeat 1 Lvl 41–50 Ascendancy Minion+1,800
  • Defeat 1 Lvl 51–60 Ascendancy Minion+2,000

The prize

Ascendancy Hall

The season's highest honour. The first guild to capture it claims massive rewards.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

What carries over

  • Resources. Your existing resources are kept.
  • Your guild. Guilds are not disbanded. Everyone stays put.
  • Guild level. Carries over.
  • Guild tech. Unlocked technologies and research in progress are kept.
  • Season 1 leftovers. Unfinished Main Missions, Strikeback stages and exploration close. Their outstanding rewards are sent automatically.

    Rules: Frontier Dynamics
    1. The chairman of the guild holding the Star Space Station in the core region becomes Star Overlord.
    2. The chairman can give the title to any guild member instead.
    3. The Star Overlord gets exclusive benefits, including unlocking the Star Treasure.
    4. Lose the space station and the title goes with it.
    5. A Star Overlord who leaves the guild loses the title; the current chairman then appoints a new one.
  • III

    Galactic RivalsThe fight for Star Overlord again, under the same five rules as Frontier Dynamics.
    Rules: Galactic Rivals
    1. The chairman of the guild holding the Star Space Station in the core region becomes Star Overlord.
    2. The chairman can give the title to any guild member instead.
    3. The Star Overlord gets exclusive benefits, including unlocking the Star Treasure.
    4. Lose the space station and the title goes with it.
    5. A Star Overlord who leaves the guild loses the title; the current chairman then appoints a new one.
  • IV
    Week 4
    Paths to DominanceGuilds fight for the top star system's Star Space Station. The winner names the Trader Prince.
    Rules: Paths to Dominance
    1. Every two weeks, traders compete for the Star Space Station of the highest-level star system. In a week with a Dominion Warzone event, this server's competition moves back one week.
    2. Each competition lasts up to 24 hours.
    3. The winning guild's chairman has 24 hours to name any guild member Trader Prince; otherwise the chairman takes the title.
    4. The Prince assigns trader positions and issues commendations. Every trader, the Prince included, can receive one commendation per cycle.
    5. Appointments and Prince Ability effects reset when the next competition begins.

How guild rewards are handed out
  1. After the season, guild rewards enter the Distribution Stage. The Chairman can assign them by each member's season performance. Anything left is handed out automatically when the stage ends.
  2. Rewards stay in their tier. The automatic hand-out goes by Season Performance Rating, to eligible members still in the guild when rewards were locked in.
  3. Members who leave or are removed before rewards lock in lose that guild's rewards. Join a new guild before distribution and you can still get its leftovers, but you rank there with a score of 0.
  4. Rewards arrive by mail. If you got none, Reward Details shows why.
  5. Guild Occupation Points = Port Occupation Points on your home server + bonus points from the Dominion Warzone.
  6. In the Dominion Warzone every guild on the winning server gets bonus Occupation Points, the Sovereign Guild extra. The losing server gets none.

The story

The war in Korell is over. Traders head for Siwenna: rich, storm-wracked, long abandoned by the Empire and now held by the Ascendancy Court. Reopen the old trade routes, take ports and resources, and capture the Ascendancy Hall to end the Court's reign, while rival guilds fight you for the same trade network.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

The Progressive FrontResistance

Siwenna's most prominent resistance: miners, industrial workers, scholars and local merchants. Their leader, Corvin Marr, is building alliances, traders included, to reopen the routes.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

The Ascendancy CourtEnemy

The ruling power. Once a regional branch of the Ascendancy, it rules by force: it holds the ports, mines and routes, occupies the Silent Beacons, blocks safe passage and taxes trade ships.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

More factions may follow.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠
