    Built up by normal attacks. A full bar fires a Champion's Active Ability.

    FleetsIn battle

  • Energy Resonance

    A fleet-wide boost when a full component set reaches a set Empowerment Level. At Full Set +12 the Flagship trails a Photon Stream.

    FleetsFlagships

F

  • Flagship

    The ship at the heart of a fleet. It sets the fleet's style and brings big bonuses and abilities.

    Fleets

    FleetsFlagships

  • Flagship Component Sets

    The bonus for fitting enough components of the same type and quality.

    Fleets

    FleetsFlagships

  • Flagship Components

    Parts that add to a Flagship's attributes. Better quality adds more.

    Fleets

    FleetsFlagships

  • Flagship Core Component

    The part that decides a Flagship's style, and so its fleet's.

    Fleets

    FleetsStyles

  • Flagship Promotion

    Spending Flagship Blueprints for a large jump in attributes and new or stronger skills.

    Fleets

    FleetsFlagships

  • Flagship Upgrade

    Spending Galactic Coins to raise a Flagship's level, for better attributes and more combat craft.

    Fleets

    FleetsFlagships

  • Fleet

    One Flagship, three Champions and a set of combat craft, sent out together.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

    Fleets

    FleetsThe fleet

  • Fleet Rating

    One fleet's fighting strength. Power, by contrast, covers your whole account.

    Fleets

    FleetsThe fleet

G

  • Glory Acquisition Efficiency

    A star system's multiplier on the Glory you earn there.

    Galaxy

  • Glory Protection

    Cuts the Glory a much stronger attacker earns. It stops protecting you at 20,000,000 Power.

    Combat

    FleetsTrader fights

H

  • Home Port

    The one Port a trader settles in. It gives buffs that grow with its level.

    Guilds

    GuildsPorts

  • Hunting Ground Star System

    A star system whose star throws out Energy Bodies in periodic storms. Some of that energy can repair ships.

    Galaxy

I

  • Interstellar Shelter

    3 hours of immunity after other traders destroy a fleet 2 times in total.

    Combat

    FleetsTrader fights

J

  • Join a Commerce Guild

    Tap the Commerce Guild icon to create a guild or pick one from the list. Some require approval, which the Chairman sets.

    Guilds

    GuildsJoining

  • Jump Point

    A Jump Anchor or Jump Relay Station, usually found in pairs. Fleets need them to cross between star systems, and cannot be steered or attacked mid-jump.

    Galaxy

K

  • Killstreak

    A run of kills on other traders, no more than 3 minutes apart, each with at least 30% of the damage dealt by you.

    Combat

    FleetsTrader fights

M

  • Minor Damage

    Damage that heals as soon as the fleet leaves the battle, with no Repair Modules.

    Combat

    FleetsDamage

  • Mothership Development

    Building up your Mothership. Its Energy Core level caps the level of your Flagships, Champions and other buildings.

    Mothership

O

  • Overlord

    The leader of a region. Korell has 6 seats, for its 4 Border Regions and 2 Core Regions.

    GuildsSeason 1 · Korell

    GuildsTitles

P

  • Planet

    A world in a star system. A monopolized Planet Port pays resource dividends to the whole guild.

    Galaxy

    GuildsPorts

  • Port

    The hub that manages a planet. A guild that captures it controls the planet.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

    Guilds

    GuildsPorts

  • Port Garrison

    Fleets stationed in a guild Port to defend it. They get Port Guard: Fleet DEF +5%.

    Guilds

    GuildsPorts

  • Position Application

    How a trader applies for one of the six positions under the Trader Prince. It needs an Energy Core of Lvl 16 or higher.

    GuildsSeason 1 · Korell

    GuildsTitles

  • Power

    Your account's total strength, added up from six things: Flagships, combat craft, Champions, Technologies, Heroic Crew Members and Buildings.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

    Fleets

    FleetsThe fleet

  • Power System

    The Mothership's electricity, made by the Energy Core from water. Above 100% load, resource buildings produce 10% less.

    Mothership

  • Prime Radiant

    A storage device holding psychohistory's research data and Hari Seldon's digital construct. The Mule is invading it; you direct heroes inside it to help Seldon and Gray fight him off.

    Story

  • Province

    A large region of space with its own government and forces. Season 1's is Korell, split into Border, Core and Abyss regions. Season 2's is Siwenna.

    GalaxySeason 1 · Korell

    Season 2Siwenna Province

R

  • Rally

    A joint attack led by one guild member on a special target, such as a Tribute Vessel or a Port.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

    Guilds

    GuildsRallies

  • Rally Up

    2 minutes of immunity from other traders after a destroyed fleet revives at the Home Port.

    Combat

    FleetsTrader fights

  • Repair

    Fixing Major Damage with Repair Modules, in the Formation screen or the Mothership's Repair Cabin.

    Combat

    FleetsDamage

S

  • Share

    Sends a target to chat as a card. Tapping the card jumps to the target.

    Controls

  • Shipyard

    Builds combat craft. The Mothership has three, one each for Kinetic, Beam and Ion.

    Mothership

    FleetsStyles

  • Single Selection & Select All

    The button above Fleet that switches between commanding one fleet and all of them at once.

    Controls

  • Skill Activation Order

    Champions use their abilities in Formation order, left to right.

    Combat

    FleetsIn battle

  • Star

    The heart of a star system. Its Star Port governs the whole system, and a guild that wins it holds a monopoly.

    Galaxy

    GuildsPorts

  • Star Level Promotion

    Spending Champion Fragments to raise a Champion's Star Level, which unlocks or awakens abilities.

    Fleets

    FleetsChampions

  • Star System

    The basic unit of the map. The closer a system is to its province's centre, the higher its level, its enemies and its Glory rate.

    Galaxy

  • Style

    Beam, Kinetic or Ion. Each counters one other, for 5% more damage: Beam beats Kinetic, Kinetic beats Ion, Ion beats Beam.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

    Fleets

    FleetsStyles

  • Subdivision

    A part of a region, made of several star systems. In Korell, the Border Region has 4, the Core Region has 2 and the Abyss Region has none.

    GalaxySeason 1 · Korell

T

  • Tap & Drag

    Tap an enemy's body or portrait to pick it as your target.

    Controls

  • Technology

    Research at the Mothership's Research Center, in 4 branches: Economy & Development, Flagships & Combat Craft, Commerce Guild Duel, and Combat Craft Modification.

    Mothership

  • Teleport

    Recalls a fleet to your Home Port. Attempts come back every 15 minutes.

    Galaxy

    GuildsPorts

  • Trader Prince

    The title guilds fight for through the Ascendancy Fortress. The Prince assigns positions and issues rewards.

    GuildsSeason 1 · Korell

    GuildsTitles

V

  • Voyage Encounter

    A chance event you may come across while exploring star systems.

    Galaxy

W

  • War Frenzy

    5 minutes of Fleet ATK +3% and a 30% Slow on your targets, after you attack another trader.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠
