What a guild gives

A Commerce Guild is a group of traders. Joining one brings resources, buffs and new features.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

Team battles
Joining unlocks Rallies: attacks you make together.
Ports
A guild can declare war on Ports. Holding one pays every member steady resource dividends and attribute bonuses.
Mutual help
Members help each other to cut construction and research times.
Guild events
Joining opens the guild's own events. The game's entry is cut off at this point; the events themselves are further down this page.

The guild page has three tabs: Overview, Members and Feature.

Season 2 keeps your guild. Guilds are not disbanded, and guild level, unlocked technologies and research in progress all carry over.

Season 2What carries over

Joining

You can found a guild or join one that another trader made.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  • Not in a guild? Tap the Commerce Guild icon in the bottom navigation bar to create one or join one.
  • Browse the guild list and tap Join. Filters and a language search help you find a fit.
  • Some guilds ask for approval; the Chairman sets that. Tap Apply to send a request. The button turns into Withdraw, which cancels it.
  • Arms Race rewards need 48 hours of membership first.
  • Switching guilds does not clear the 5‑day cooldown from Defend the Trade Route.

Guilds can advertise too: the Recruitment screen has a Publish Recruitment button and a recommended player of the day.

Titles and positions

Three kinds of title sit above ordinary membership. Guilds fight for two of them, Trader Prince and Overlord; traders apply for the positions.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

Season 1The game's Encyclopedia describes these for Season 1, in Korell. Season 2's own rule screens decide the Trader Prince at the highest-level star system's Star Space Station, and name a Star Overlord for the core region.

Season 2Week by week

Trader Prince

  • Guilds compete for the title through ownership of the Ascendancy Fortress.
  • The winning guild's Chairman can name any guild member Trader Prince. With no appointment, the Chairman takes the title.
  • The Prince assigns trader positions, issues rewards and holds other privileges.

The six positions

The Trader Prince oversees six positions:

  1. Acting Prince
  2. Strategic Counselor
  3. Military Counselor
  4. Construction Counselor
  5. Research Counselor
  6. Counselor of Internal…

Not shown yetThe sixth title is cut off in the capture.

  • Each position gives bonuses in its own field.
  • The Acting Prince can also approve or appoint the other positions, but not the Trader Prince or the Acting Prince.
  • Any trader with an Energy Core of Lvl 16 or higher can apply. The Trader Prince or the Acting Prince confirms each appointment.

Overlord

  • Korell has 6 Overlord seats: one for each of its 4 Border Regions and 2 Core Regions.
  • A guild that holds a Lvl 4 Star Port in a Border Region, or a Lvl 7 Star Port in a Core Region, can have its Chairman name any member Overlord of that region.
  • A trader can hold only one Overlord title at a time.
  • Overlords get more Fleet Attack and a special emblem.
  • Stellar Treasure. Traders whose Home Port is in the Overlord's region charge it by finishing Trader Commissions. When it is full the Overlord unlocks it, and everyone who helped is rewarded.

Ports and garrisons

Ports are how a guild holds territory. A Port manages a planet: capture it and you control the planet.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

Declaring war
A guild can declare war on any Port except the neutral Ports of the starting star system. Win, and the guild occupies it.
Star Ports
A star's Star Port governs its whole star system. Win the war and the guild holds a monopoly: steady resource dividends and benefits for every member, and the system joins the guild's controlled regions.
Planet Ports
A monopolized Planet Port pays steady resource dividends to every member.

Home Port

  • Each trader settles in one Planet Port and sets it as Home Port. The game names neutral Ports in the starting star system and Ports your guild occupies; its entry is cut off after that.
  • A Home Port gives buffs that grow with its level: a Construction Speed Bonus, and its level sets your Trade Shipping Level.
  • The Home Port button, bottom right of the map, jumps to it.
  • To bring a fleet home, tap the Home Port, then Recall. Teleport attempts come back every 15 minutes.

Garrison

  • Station fleets in your guild's Port to defend it against other guilds.
  • The first fleet in becomes garrison leader and gives battle buffs. The leader can hand the role on. Guild admins can change the leader or remove fleets.
  • Garrisoned fleets get Port Guard: Fleet DEF +5%.
  • If one garrisoned fleet enters War Frenzy in combat, every fleet garrisoned at that Port gets it. It does not vanish at once when a fleet leaves.

Rallies

A rally is a joint attack led by one guild member on a special target, such as a Tribute Vessel or a Port.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  • A rally fleet adds up every participant's attributes. Only the initiator's buffs and Champion abilities apply.
  • The War screen's Rally tab lists open rallies, each with a Join button.

Auto-join

  • Switch it on in the settings on the Rally page.
  • Your fleets then return to the Home Port and wait to join rallies that guild members start.
  • Targets that allow auto-join: Tribute Vessels, Ports and Deliverer Arks.
  • Fleets in auto-join are repaired automatically. The game's entry is cut off after that.

Chests, technology and Legacy

What the guild earns together comes back to its members.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

Chests

  • Winning a rallied battle grants Loot Chests: defeating a Tribute Vessel, or any NPC enemy that needs a rally, gives them.
  • Common, Rare and Epic Guild Chests fill up from chest points. The Rare bar reads out of 300, the Epic bar out of 900.
  • A pack that includes Guild Chests also gives one to each member of your guild. The game's line is cut off after that.

Technology

  • Commerce Guild Technology has two tabs: Fast Development and Space Combat.
  • Unlocked guild technologies and research in progress carry into Season 2.

Guild Legacy

  • Guild Legacy takes donations: the screen shows Level 0 going to Level 1, and donation attempts run on a cooldown.

Guild events

The guild's event list holds Arms Race, Defend the Trade Route and Star of the Guild, with more promised. Battle Trial sits in the main event bar and calls on the guild in its Elite stages.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

Escort

Defend the Trade Route

The Ascendancy is after the guild's Cargo Dreadnought. Beat its forces and escort the ship to its destination. The more enemy fleets you destroy, the better the rewards.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  1. 01Starting Point
  2. 02Waypoint A3‑minute rest
  3. 03Waypoint B3‑minute rest
  4. 04Destination
  1. Preparation. A 3‑minute prep time. Traders gather in the Prep Area on the event map.
  2. Escort. The dreadnought sets off with at least 1 trader Flagship as escort. Enemies attack all the way. Rally to defend the ship, or go out and hunt the threats.
  3. Waypoints. At each of the first two waypoints the ship rests for 3 minutes. Enemies may set an ambush for the next leg.

If you cannot be there

  • Set one Flagship to Auto-Process. While you are offline, the game joins for you and escorts with that Flagship.
  • Delegated traders get the same rewards as those who play.
  • After a delegation that worked you can still join by hand, but the game will not take over again.
  • The same rules page puts participation and reward eligibility on a 5‑day cooldown, which stays even if you switch guilds. What starts it is cut off in the capture.
  • At the start of a new round, members can appoint a Flagship to the guild's Escort Fleet in advance.

Damage race

Arms Race

Near the Commerce Guild Hub, the guild fights enemies set up by the Galactic Trade Consortium. Guild admins start it by hand or by reservation.

  • Stages A and B
  • 30 minutes
  • 5 waves
  1. Morale first. Guild members defeat Tribute Vessels to raise the Morale Level. Each level reached brings a defense reduction and rewards. Morale Level 3 needs 50 Tribute Vessels.
  2. Stage A locks in the current Morale Level and applies its bonuses.
  3. Stage B can be started within 24 hours after Stage A ends. It inherits Stage A's benefits and difficulty.
  4. The event lasts 30 minutes and has 5 waves of Drill Cruisers. Beat every wave to unlock higher difficulties.
  5. If the Highest Damage Rewards are already reached after Stage A, the Chairman can skip the wait and hand out rewards at once. Stage B then cannot be started.
  6. Morale bonuses must be earned again after each event.

Rewards

  • You need 48 hours in the guild to be eligible.
  • Damage from Stage A and Stage B is added up. Rewards come by mail after the round.
  • There are two reward tabs, Commerce Guild Rewards and Individual Rewards. The higher the top player's damage, the better the rewards for the whole guild.

Level 1 bands for your personal best in a round:

  • 750K–1B
  • 550K–750K
  • 200K–550K
  • 1–200K

Weekly awards

Star of the Guild

Weekly awards for the members of the server's strongest guilds.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  1. Every Monday at 00:00 UTC, the top 10 Commerce Guilds in Power qualify. They unlock the event that Sunday, even if the ranking changes later.
  2. Awards go out based on the week's guild activity.
  3. The Chairman picks the Honorable Trader. The system decides every other award by its rule.
  4. Each award is announced after a 1‑hour wait. Then every guild member gets one free like to give, which can earn chests.
  5. A Star of the Guild earns contribution points for the guild, by performance and server ranking. More points unlock bigger dividends. Leave the guild before the event starts and your Contribution Points are cleared. Once it begins, the week's results are locked.

When the last reward is revealed, Bonus Cargo Holds appear around the Commerce Guild Hub. Their number and quality depend on a total that is cut off in the capture.

There is one edition every Sunday: Edition 18 fell on Nov 8, 2026.

The awards

  • Star of TriumphMost Dominion Warzone points in the week
  • Star of DefenseHighest damage in Defend the Trade Route that week
  • Star of CompetitionNot shown yet

  • Star of EndeavorMost Action Points used in the week

28 levels

Battle Trial

A contest between the Captain and Javier over who brings down the most Ascendancy Judicators. You climb 28 levels.

  1. Pick one difficulty for each event. You cannot change it during the event.
  2. Clear all 28 levels of your difficulty and higher difficulties unlock in the next Trial Event.
  3. In Elite challenges you can start a rally and call guild members in to help.
