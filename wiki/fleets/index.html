What a fleet is

A fleet is 1 Flagship, 3 Champions and a set of combat craft.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  1. FlagshipSets the fleet's style×1
  2. ChampionsAbilities fire left to right×3
  3. Combat craftGrows with Flagship level
  • Set up your Flagships, Champions and combat craft on the Fleet page. Its button is on the left of the home screen.
  • How many fleets you can send out grows with your Energy Core level.
  • How many combat craft a fleet carries grows with its Flagship's level. The game's example adds one more craft at level 11.
  • Match your Champions to the Flagship's style and the fleet gets a bonus: see Style Resonance.

Power and Fleet Rating

Power covers your whole account. It adds up six things: Flagships, combat craft, Champions, Technologies, Heroic Crew Members and Buildings. Your total sits at the top left of the home screen. Tap it for the breakdown.

Fleet Rating is one fleet's fighting strength. It counts the Flagship you deployed, its combat craft and Champions, plus your Technologies, Heroic Crew Members and certain Buildings.

  • Compare both sides' Fleet Ratings on a target's profile panel.
  • The Formation screen breaks your own rating down.
  • The game grades each part of your Power against your Energy Core level. Raising the lowest-graded parts is the efficient way to grow Power.

The four attributes

HP
Maximum health. At 0 the fleet is defeated.
ATK
Raises the damage you deal, INT damage aside.
DEF
Cuts the damage you take, INT damage aside.
INT
Raises the INT damage you deal and cuts the INT damage you take.

Styles and counters

Every Flagship, Champion and combat craft belongs to one of three styles: Beam, Kinetic or Ion.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  • Beam counters Kinetic. Kinetic counters Ion. Ion counters Beam.
  • Hit a style you counter and you deal 5% more damage.
  • The Flagship's style is the fleet's style. The Flagship's Core Component sets it, so swapping the Core Component changes the whole fleet's style.
  • Three Shipyards on the Mothership build the combat craft, one for each style. Better craft unlock as a Shipyard levels up.

Style Resonance

Put Champions of the Flagship's style in the fleet, and the Flagship and the combat craft of that style get stronger.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

The game's panel lists these two steps and shows them for a Beam Flagship.

Champions

Champions command the fleet in space combat: they add attributes and fire abilities. On the ground you steer them yourself, in landing battles and Prime Radiant stages.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

Rarity
Epic or Legendary. Legendary Champions are harder to get.
Where they come from
Venturous Recruitment, quest rewards, events and packs. These give Champions or Champion Fragments.
Style
Beam, Kinetic or Ion. A Champion only reaches full strength in a fleet of the same style.
Role
Attack, Defense, Healing or Support. Each does a different job in battle.

Tags on a Champion's card

Attack
Built to deal heavy damage for the formation.
Limited
Marks the galaxy's famed figures.

The card has three tabs: Info, Ability and Star Level.

Making a Champion stronger

  • LevelSpendFood

    Higher base attributes, and more Command Points for the fleet.

  • Star LevelSpendChampion Fragments

    A big jump in base attributes. At certain Star Levels, abilities unlock or awaken and their level caps rise.

  • AbilitiesSpendTraining Manuals

    Stronger abilities. The Star Level decides which abilities can unlock or awaken, and how far they can go.

The Energy Core caps it all: its level sets the highest level your Flagships, Champions and other buildings can reach.

Flagships

The Flagship is the heart of a fleet. It brings large attribute bonuses and abilities of its own.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

Flagship Blueprints come from attacking Tribute Vessels, from events and from packs.

  • UpgradeSpendGalactic Coins

    Raises the Flagship's level: higher base attributes, and room for more combat craft.

  • PromotionSpendFlagship Blueprints

    A large jump in base attributes. It also unlocks or improves the Flagship's skills.

Components

Flagship Components
Add to the Flagship's base attributes. Better quality adds more. Buy them in the Glory Shop, or win them from Ascendancy Shrines and Tribute Vessels.
Component sets
Fit enough components of the same type and quality and a set bonus switches on.
Core Component
Decides the Flagship's style, and so the fleet's. Swap it to counter an enemy.
Energy Resonance
Prismatic Cores empower Legendary components. When a full set reaches a set Empowerment Level, Energy Resonance adds more to the fleet's attributes. At Full Set +12, a Photon Stream trails the Flagship in flight.

Crew and the Echo Hub

Crew come in two kinds, Heroic and Construction. Most come from Crew Member Recruitment.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  • Open the Crew Roster by tapping the Energy Core on the Mothership. There you raise crew star levels and assign crew.
  • Construction Crew Members work in the Mothership's buildings. They add production boosts and abilities.
  • Heroic Crew Members go to the Echo Hub. Each is assigned to one of three styles and adds combat bonuses and abilities to fleets of that style.

The Echo Hub

  • Unlocks when your Energy Core reaches Lvl 20.
  • You start with one assignment slot. Enhancing the hub unlocks more slots and more Computation Nexuses.
  • Each Nexus takes only one Heroic Crew Member of the same profession.
  • The hub has 10 Enhancement Stages, each made of several Nodes. Enhance Nodes with Echo Modules, then break through with Echoes of Deep Space. Finish every Node in a stage to move to the next.
  • Professions seen on the hub: Warrior, Sage, Consultant and Guardian.

In space combat, the side with more Command Points deals extra damage.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

In battle

Three small rules decide how a fight plays out.

Skill order
Champions use their abilities in the order they stand in the Formation, left to right. Reorder them to get the most from each ability.
Energy
Normal attacks build Energy. A full bar fires the Champion's Active Ability.
Counterattack
A fleet that is attacked hits back. That damage shows under the normal attack damage.

Damage and repair

Damage to a fleet is part Minor and part Major. The split depends on what you are fighting.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

Minor Damage
Heals at once when the fleet leaves the battle. It costs no Repair Modules.
Major Damage
Needs Repair Modules. Repair in the Formation screen, from the formation list on the left of the screen, or in the Mothership's Repair Cabin.
Destroyed
In long battles, too much Major Damage can destroy combat craft. Destroyed craft are gone for good.

Fighting other traders

Fights between traders come with effects of their own.

War Frenzy
Attack another trader and every fleet in the attack gets War Frenzy for 5 minutes: Fleet ATK +3%, and your attacks slow the target by 30%. Triggering it again restarts the clock.
Rally Up
A destroyed fleet comes back at your Home Port after a wait, with Rally Up for 2 minutes. Other traders cannot attack it. Attacking a trader yourself ends it.
Interstellar Shelter
Destroyed by other traders 2 times in total, a fleet gets Interstellar Shelter for 3 hours, and no trader can attack it. Attacking a trader yourself ends it and resets the count.
Glory Protection
Attack a much weaker trader and you earn less Glory; the cut grows with the gap between your Flagships and combat craft and theirs. From 20,000,000 Power up, you no longer get this protection when others attack you.
Killstreak
Defeat traders back to back to build a Killstreak. The target must be online, you must deal at least 30% of the damage it takes, and kills can be no more than 3 minutes apart. From 10 kills in a row the broadcast gets grander.

Valor Arena

A ticketed ladder: pick an opponent, win Points and climb the ranking.⁠‌‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌‌​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​‌⁠

  1. An Arena Ticket starts a challenge. Tickets refill to 3 at 00:00 UTC each day. Out of tickets? Buy more with Credits.
  2. Each challenge offers three opponents. Pick one. Beating it earns Points.
  3. Win or lose, every challenge pays Valor Coins. Spend them in the Valor Shop.
  4. When the event ends, Settlement Rewards go out by your final points and rank. If members of your Commerce Guild place high on the leaderboard, everyone in the guild also gets Commerce Guild Rewards.
  5. At the Peerless Master tier you also get a detailed battle report after each match.

Tiers seen so far: Radiant Diamond and Peerless Master.
