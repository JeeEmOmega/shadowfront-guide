# Shadowfront Battle Guide

Our guild's battle plan for **Shadowfront** in *Foundation: Galactic Frontier*.

**Open the guide:** https://jeeemomega.github.io/shadowfront-guide/

- The three team roles on the real Vault map. Pick our starting corner and every map, target and call follows it.
- Win the count, when to turn on a whale, the outer-rim tip, side pressure, Awakening moves and timed takedowns.
- Tap-to-copy calls for guild chat, and a per-role field card.
- **Team plans:** https://jeeemomega.github.io/shadowfront-guide/teams/ shows where every fleet goes in each of a squad's 3 teams (10 + 5 + 5 players), by role code, with what to do when things change.
- **Roster & log (for commanders):** https://jeeemomega.github.io/shadowfront-guide/roster/ fills both squads each week by power, makes a link that shows players their names on the maps in their own language, and keeps a weekly log on your device.
- 13 languages. Every page opens in your phone's language, or add `#lang-es`, `#lang-pt`, `#lang-fr`, `#lang-it`, `#lang-de`, `#lang-cs`, `#lang-ru`, `#lang-tr`, `#lang-id`, `#lang-zh`, `#lang-ja` or `#lang-ko` to the link.

Unofficial fan guide. Not made by or affiliated with the game's developer or publisher.
